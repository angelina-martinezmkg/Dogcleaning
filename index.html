About us

Bath time, minus the drama.

We're a dog cleaning service built around one simple idea: a bath should feel good for your dog, not just look good when they leave. We take our time, read their mood, and keep things calm, warm and patient from the first rinse to the final fluff.

Big dogs, small dogs, double coats, curly coats, nervous first-timers and seasoned spa regulars: they all get the same gentle routine. We'll talk through what your dog needs, recommend only what helps, and send them home clean, comfortable and smelling great.
